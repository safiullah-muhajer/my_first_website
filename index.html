History

Ankara University was officially established in 1946. Some of its faculties began their activities before the university was founded. It has played an important role in the development of higher education and scientific research in Türkiye.

Faculties and Departments

Ankara University offers education in many academic fields, including science, medicine, law, languages, and social sciences. Its departments help students develop their knowledge and professional skills.

Research and Innovation

Research is an important part of Ankara University's academic activities. Students and researchers work in different fields to produce new knowledge and contribute to scientific progress.

Student Life

Students can participate in academic events, cultural activities, sports, and student clubs. These opportunities help students develop new skills, meet others, and enjoy university life.
